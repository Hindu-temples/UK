Hindu festivals & events in Cambridge

Cambridge has a large and fast-growing Hindu community — and no Hindu temple. The nearest mandir is over 25 miles away, so families here celebrate Diwali, Navratri, Ganesh Chaturthi and Holi in hired halls and community centres, organised by volunteers. A campaign led by Hindu Samaj Northstowe, a registered charity, is working to change that and secure a permanent mandir for Cambridgeshire. Cambridge needs a temple; until it has one, this page gathers the festival events happening across the city in one place, so nobody has to miss out simply because the information was scattered.

Festival dates this season

National dates. Groups in Cambridge may observe on a nearby day — check each event above.

  • 14 September 2026Ganesh ChaturthiGanesha is welcomed into homes and mandirs.
  • 25 September 2026Anant Chaturdashi / Ganesh VisarjanVisarjan in the UK usually uses tanks or eco-friendly clay murtis — immersion in open water needs permission.
  • 11–19 October 2026Sharad NavratriNine nights. Garba in the UK is often held in community halls rather than the mandir.
  • 20 October 2026Dussehra (Vijayadashami)Sources differ: most give Maha Navami on 19 Oct and Vijayadashami on 20 Oct. Check with your temple.
  • 29 October 2026Karwa Chauth
  • 6 November 2026DhanterasFirst day of the five-day Diwali period.
  • 8 November 2026Diwali (Lakshmi Puja)Main day. UK temples do not all observe on the same date — check with your temple.
  • 9 November 2026Govardhan Puja / Annakut / Gujarati New YearSources differ between 9 and 10 November. Annakut is offered the day after Diwali at most UK mandirs.
  • 24 November 2026Kartik PurnimaDate not independently verified — confirm before relying on it.
